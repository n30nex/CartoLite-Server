# Pachinko

Open `/pachinko/` from Experimental. Each new sanitized packet becomes one dot; no links are drawn. Its category selects an Advert, Trace, Text, ACK, Control or Other lane. Dots bounce around pegs, settle in the matching bucket and overflow into the drain. Positions and collisions are an illustration, not geography, coverage, signal strength or radio travel.

Drag to orbit; right-drag or two fingers pan; pinch or scroll zoom. Arrow keys orbit and Shift-arrows pan. Home resets the camera. Pause stops new dots, physics and sound. Drain opens the bucket floors briefly; Clear immediately empties the session. Hidden, paused and replayed traffic is never backfilled. Every session starts empty.

Click a dot or use Find to inspect its public observations. Sound remains opt-in. Shared display settings control appearance and motion; packet colors stay bright against the dark machine. A bounded dot/queue budget protects long sessions, and omitted events are disclosed.

Worldwide operators must enable `pachinko` in `EXTRA_PAGES` and use the matching Compose file. Labs remains Canada-only. [Generated materials and prompts](scene-assets.md).

Camera offers **Angle**, **Front** and **Above** views; the default angle keeps
the lanes readable. With the scene focused, **Space** pauses or resumes. Colored
lane rails and compact peg flashes distinguish incoming activity. Pause preserves
light timing, and clearing the session clears its pulses too. A drained packet
keeps readable observed details with a clear drained status.
